The platform

bankrewards.io

Website creator · 2023 – present

Bank, credit-card, and brokerage bonus comparison platform. I built the entire thing end to end: a SvelteKit site on Cloudflare Workers (D1), a Payload CMS backend, an LLM-agent pipeline that continuously re-verifies every offer against banks' live pages, and a Discord bot for offer intake and weekly review.

Why: bank bonuses are free money that most people leave on the table because comparing them is tedious. I wanted to automate the tedious part.

Apps & games

Noncents

UW · 2022

A card game about outsmarting others with trades and investments that makes finance fun. It was designed to teach market and personal-finance basics to players 12 and up. Built with fellow UW students; I worked on the Next.js site with the how-to-play guide and ordering.

Why: making finance fun enough that a 12-year-old plays it voluntarily, and shipping a physical product instead of just code.

Tally Diary

Diary app with 5,000+ users across iOS, Android, Windows, and Web. It's a React PWA storing diaries in personal cloud storage under password protection.

Why: I wanted a diary that lived everywhere I do, without handing my entries to a company.

Triangul8

A physical board game of triangular bakelite tiles. I worked on the Unity port that brought it to iOS and Android. Featured by GeekWire as a modern-day "chess killer."

Why: taking a real, tactile game and making it sing on a touchscreen.
